NEO P2 — UPDATE FILES
=====================

Practice 2 extends the Neo language processor developed in Practice 1.

IMPORTANT
---------

Do not replace your lexer.py or parser.py files.

You must continue working from your own implementation of Practice 1
and extend it according to the Practice 2 specification.


UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------

1. Start from your completed Practice 1 project.

2. Replace the following files with the versions provided in this package:

   neo.py
   compiler/ast_nodes.py

3. Add the following new file:

   compiler/evaluator.py

4. Keep your existing files:

   compiler/lexer.py
   compiler/parser.py

5. Extend your lexer and parser according to the Practice 2 statement.


FINAL PROJECT STRUCTURE
-----------------------

Neo/
├── neo.py
└── compiler/
    ├── __init__.py
    ├── ast_nodes.py
    ├── evaluator.py
    ├── lexer.py
    └── parser.py


FILES TO MODIFY
---------------

Students must modify only:

    compiler/lexer.py
    compiler/parser.py

The remaining files provide the Neo execution environment and
must not be modified.
